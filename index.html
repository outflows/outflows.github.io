Welcome!

I am a postdoctoral researcher at INAF - Osservatorio Astronomico di Brera, working with Dr. Lara Nava at the High Energy Astrophysics Group. My main interests are related to high-energy astrophysics, compact objects, and transient events. Before moving to INAF, I worked with Prof. Davide Lazzati at Oregon State University. I obtained my PhD in Astronomy at the Institute of Astronomy, Geophysics and Atmospheric Sciences of the University of São Paulo, where I worked under supervision of Prof. Rodrigo Nemmen.

Outside of science, my interests and hobbies include books, climbing, hiking, and drums.
