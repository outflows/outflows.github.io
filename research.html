Research

In general terms, I am interested in high-energy astrophysics, compact objects, and transient events - especially when these three things can be combined! So far, most of my work has been on the computational and modeling side of things, but I am becoming more and more interested in the data analysis/observational aspects of these phenomena. Currently, my focus is on gamma-ray bursts (GRB). Some topics I'm interested in include:

  • Prompt GRB emission mechanisms and particle acceleration
  • Fundamental differences between short and long GRBs, based on prompt and afterglow properties
  • Physics of compact objects (black holes and neutron stars)
  • Magnetohydrodynamics of accretion flows and relativistic jets
  • Electromagnetic counterparts of gravitational waves
  • Shock formation, evolution and propagation in different astrophysical scenarios
  • High-performance computing, algorithm development
